How to open Text Shot

Text Shot isn’t notarized by Apple, so macOS may show a warning even though the app is safe. This is a one-time setup and takes less than a minute.

  1. Open the installer, then drag Text Shot into your Applications folder.

  2. Open Terminal and paste this command:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Text Shot.app"

Simple updates — once installed, check for new releases with one click.
